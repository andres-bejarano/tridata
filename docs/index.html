TriData

A personal, local-first training and nutrition app for one triathlete.

TriData runs on your own PC. It brings your Garmin Connect history into one local file, tracks what you eat, and puts an AI coach on top of training, recovery and nutrition at once. You use it from your phone, on your own network.

Google Calendar

If you connect it in Settings › Google Calendar, TriData creates its own calendar called «TriData» in your Google account and keeps it up to date with the sessions of your training plan and the trainings you have done. It only sees and changes that calendar: Google's permission (calendar.app.created) does not let it read or touch any other calendar or event.

Your data

Everything stays on your PC. There is no TriData server, account or telemetry. Read the privacy policy.
