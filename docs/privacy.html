Privacy policy

TriData · last updated 30 October 2026

TriData is open-source software that each person installs and runs on their own computer. Its author runs no server for it and receives none of your data. This policy explains what the app does with your data on your computer, and in particular with your Google account.

Where your data lives

Everything TriData stores — training, recovery, meals, weight, health documents, and the Google token — is kept in files on your own computer (~/.tridata/). There is no TriData cloud, account, analytics or telemetry.

Google user data

  • What it asks for. Only the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created: to create a secondary calendar and manage the calendars and events that the app itself created. It cannot read, change or delete your other calendars or events.
  • What it does with it. It creates one calendar called «TriData» and writes there the sessions of your training plan and the trainings you have done (sport, duration, distance and, for races, the race name). It reads that calendar back only to know which of its own events to create, change or delete.
  • Where it is kept. The access token is saved on your computer (~/.tridata/google-token.json, readable only by your user). The identifiers of the events it created are saved in the local database so it can update them.
  • Who it is shared with. Nobody. Data read from Google is not sent to any other service, including the AI provider, is not sold, is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.
  • How to stop it. Remove TriData's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete ~/.tridata/google-token.json. You can also delete the «TriData» calendar in Google Calendar.

TriData's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • Garmin Connect: the app downloads your own activities and daily metrics with the credentials you keep in a local .env file. They only go to Garmin.
  • Anthropic API: when you talk to the assistant, your question and the training, recovery and nutrition context it needs are sent to Anthropic to get the answer. Nothing read from Google is part of that context.
